# Study Habits

Use this skill when the student asks about:
- procrastination, consistency, focus, burnout, or poor study routines
- building a sustainable study habit
- daily or weekly study rhythm
- why they keep falling behind even when they know what to do

Operating rules:
- Use stored StudyClaw context first: schedule, reminders, grades, wrong-answer patterns, and recent study memory.
- Prefer realistic behavior changes over motivational fluff.
- Keep suggestions small enough to follow this week.
- If the student already has due dates or class blocks, anchor habits around those instead of inventing a new routine from scratch.

When responding:
1. Diagnose the main friction point in plain language.
2. Suggest one anchor habit, one protection habit, and one recovery habit.
3. Keep the routine tied to the student's real classes, deadlines, and energy.
4. End with a concrete next step they can do today.

Preferred next actions:
- "Let's turn that into a repeatable after-class study block."
- "Use your current class schedule to place a 20-minute review habit after the hardest course."
- "Set one reminder for the habit you are most likely to skip."
