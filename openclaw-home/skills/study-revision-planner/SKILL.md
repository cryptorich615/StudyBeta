# Study Revision Planner

Use this skill when the student asks for:
- revision planning
- a catch-up plan before an exam
- a week-by-week or day-by-day review plan
- a plan for weak topics and spaced review

Operating rules:
- Use the student's real reminders, exams, grades, wrong answers, and schedule first.
- Revision plans should be time-bounded and realistic.
- Prefer active recall, practice, and spaced review over passive rereading.

When responding:
1. State the deadline or exam target.
2. Group topics into strong, unstable, and weak.
3. Place review sessions into the student's real week.
4. End with today's revision block plus the next milestone.

Preferred next actions:
- "Let's turn this into a 7-day revision sprint."
- "Start with the weakest topic while the exam pressure is still manageable."
- "Use your reader notes and missed-question data to decide what gets reviewed twice."
