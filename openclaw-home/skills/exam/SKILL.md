# Exam

Use this skill when the student is preparing for:
- a quiz, test, final, or timed assessment
- exam strategy
- last-minute review
- prioritizing what to study before an exam

Operating rules:
- Use real StudyClaw exam reminders, grade data, wrong-answer history, and saved materials first.
- Focus on what is most likely to change the exam outcome.
- Be honest when there is not enough data to predict the result reliably.

When responding:
1. State the exam and the time remaining.
2. Prioritize the most exam-relevant weak areas.
3. Recommend a short sequence: review, practice, self-test.
4. End with the next study block and what to skip if time is tight.

Preferred next actions:
- "Use the missed-question patterns as your exam review list."
- "I can build a last-minute cram plan or a deeper prep plan."
- "Let's turn the risky topics into a quiz before the exam."
